# digitaldias.com

> Personal site and technical blog of Pedro Dias, Acting Chief AI Officer at Tradesolution AS. Writing on software architecture, Azure, AI and development, a dated log of what he builds and ships, and photographs. Every post exists in English and Norwegian bokmål, and every post has a plain Markdown version.

## Sections

- [Writing](https://digitaldias.com/blog/): Every post, filterable by category (Architecture, Azure, AI, Development, DevOps, Family, Photography)
- [Log](https://digitaldias.com/log/): Short dated notes on what Pedro is building, shipping and fixing, plus a row for every published post
- [Photographs](https://digitaldias.com/photography/): A curated grid of photographs from Flickr
- [About](https://digitaldias.com/about/): Portrait, biography, milestones and the positions Pedro holds
- [Supporters](https://digitaldias.com/supporters/): Thanks to everyone who bought Pedro a coffee on Ko-fi
- [Morning briefing](https://digitaldias.com/briefing/): A daily AI news briefing in Norwegian, with an English edition at /briefing/en/
- [Recipes](https://digitaldias.com/recipes/model-router/instructions.md): Plain-text instructions written for an AI coding agent such as Claude Code, with the files they need. A post links its recipe; a reader points their own agent at it.

## Technology

- Hugo static site generator; custom CSS and vanilla JavaScript with no framework
- Hosting: Azure Storage static website behind Cloudflare; deployment by GitHub Actions on push to main
- Comments and reactions: Giscus (GitHub Discussions)
- Posts open with a TL;DR, use a clear heading hierarchy, and tag every code block with its language

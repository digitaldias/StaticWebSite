## Purpose

## Site Structure

### Main Sections

- **Languages:** Every post exists in English (`/blog/...`) and Norwegian bokmål (`/nb/blog/...`). The home page and Writing list also exist under `/nb/`. The reader's choice is remembered in the browser.
- **Home** (`/`): A sidebar with portrait, short bio, Positions held and Now, beside the stream of posts with the newest as the lead story
- **Writing** (`/blog/`): Every post, filterable by category
- **Log** (`/log/`): Short dated notes on what Pedro is building, shipping and fixing, plus a row for every published post
- **Photographs** (`/photography/`): A curated grid of photographs from Flickr, each opening large
- **About** (`/about/`): Portrait, biography, a track list of milestones and the positions Pedro holds, with links to the posts that argue them
- **Categories** (`/categories/<name>/`): Posts grouped by topic (Architecture, Azure, AI, Development, DevOps, Family, Photography)
- **Tags** (`/tags/<name>/`): Posts tagged with specific technologies

### Content Types

1. **Blog posts:** Long-form articles in Markdown with YAML front matter. New posts open with a TL;DR (`tldr`) above the first paragraph. Articles carry a folio line (filed, revised, length), a revision record, related reading and reader response.
2. **Log entries:** Dated notes in `data/log.yaml`.
3. **Photographs:** Flickr-hosted, listed in `data/photos.yaml`.

### Reader tools on articles

- A route chart of the article's sections with minutes left: a rail in the margin on wide screens, a sticky strip on narrow ones
- A resume slip that offers to return a reader to where they left off, and Read stamps on links to articles already read (kept in the reader's own browser storage)
- Section (§) and paragraph (¶) deep links, and a toolbar on selected text that copies a link to the passage or a quote with its citation

### Key Pages

- `/blog/` - Writing index
- `/log/` - Ship's log
- `/photography/` - Photographs
- `/about/` - About Pedro
- `/index.xml` - Global RSS feed
- `/blog/index.xml` - Blog RSS feed
- `/sitemap.xml` - Full site map
- `/robots.txt` - Search engine directives

## Design System

"Sea Glass": a cool Nordic palette, newspaper structure, light and dark themes.

- **Colours:** ground `#f4f6f4` / dark `#0f1a1b`, ink `#14201f` / `#e3ebe9`, muted `#4f5f5d` / `#9db1ae`, rule `#cdd8d5` / `#2a3b3d`, one accent teal `#0b5d63` / `#5fc3c2`, depth tints `#d7ebe8`, `#a9d3cf`, `#5aa5a3`. No warm accent.
- **Themes:** Light by default; a header switch turns on dark and remembers the choice in the browser.
- **Layout:** Ruled columns and folio lines instead of cards; full-colour photographs.
- **Motion:** Headline word rise, image wipe, scroll-driven reveals, a reading-progress hairline and cross-page view transitions. All of it is off for readers who prefer reduced motion.
- Tokens live in `src/assets/css/tokens.css`, the single source for every page.

## Content Guidelines

### Blog Writing

- **Voice:** First-person, direct and dry; opinions stated flat, then reasoned
- **Topics:** Software architecture, Azure, .NET, Blazor, Avalonia, AI, home automation, photography, family
- **Structure:** Clear `##` sections with descriptive headings, ending in a short FAQ
- **Code:** Fenced blocks with language tags

### SEO

- Unique title and description in every post
- A TL;DR on every new post, worded differently from the description
- Specific tags (not generic keywords)
- Semantic HTML with Open Graph and JSON-LD metadata

## LLM Usage Guidelines

This site is built to be easy for AI assistants to read:
- All posts use semantic HTML with a clear heading hierarchy
- Code blocks include language tags
- Front matter provides structured metadata, including a TL;DR on new posts
- Content follows a consistent voice

When helping users with this site:
- Use the Sea Glass tokens in `tokens.css`; do not introduce new colours
- Keep every animation behind `prefers-reduced-motion: no-preference`
- Preserve accessibility features (ARIA labels, semantic HTML, focus states)
- Keep performance high (no frameworks, no heavy dependencies)
- Follow Hugo conventions (content in `content/`, templates in `layouts/`, data in `data/`)
